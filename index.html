🎧 Customer Service

My item arrived broken. Can I return it?

Yes. After you confirm the order as received, open My Purchases, find the order and tap Return broken item. Describe the problem and add a photo if you can. You have 7 days after receiving the item. If approved, you are refunded.

How do I pay for my order?

After the seller confirms your order it moves to To Pay. Open My Purchases and choose GCash, Cash on Delivery or Credit Card.

Why can't I see or buy 18+ items?

18+ items are hidden from guests and from accounts under 18. If your date of birth shows you are 18 or older, open Age Verification in the sidebar and send a photo of your National ID plus a selfie holding it. Once a store admin approves it, 18+ items appear.

I forgot my password.

On the Sign In window tap Forgot password? and verify with your email and date of birth.

How do I contact the store directly?

Email bruhboyxean30@gmail.com or call 0917-123-4567. Or send a ticket here and reply from My Tickets.
